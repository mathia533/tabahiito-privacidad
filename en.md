# Tabahiito Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026.

Tabahiito is an interval timer for Android. It works offline and without accounts.

## What data is stored
Your workouts, session history, settings and session recovery checkpoint are stored **only on your phone**. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer has no access to this data. The only information that leaves your phone is what you choose to share, as explained below.

## Error log
The app stores a rotating technical error log on your phone, up to 128 KiB. Each line contains only the date, app version, device model and error type. It does not include workouts, history, names, identifiers, error messages or paths. It is not sent automatically: in **Settings > About > Share error log**, you can share the file using an app of your choice. That app and its policy determine where it goes. If you want the developer to receive it, you must send it yourself.

## Contact from the app
“Contact” opens an email draft containing the app version and device model. The app does not send it: the email and anything you write are handled by your email app and are not added to the log.

## Permissions and how they are used
- **Vibration:** to vibrate at phase changes, if you enable it.
- **Keep the phone awake (WAKE_LOCK):** to keep time and alerts running with the screen locked during a session.
- **Foreground service:** keeps the session you started running while you use another app or lock the screen. It always follows your action and appears in a notification.
- **Notifications:** to show the session status and the Pause, Resume and Stop buttons. This is optional.
- **Phone state (READ_PHONE_STATE):** to detect a call and pause the session. This is optional. The app does not read phone numbers, contacts or call content, and does not store any of that information.

## Voice
Voice alerts use your phone’s text-to-speech engine and only installed voices that work offline in Spanish, English or Portuguese. A voice matching your language and region is preferred. If it is unavailable, another supported offline voice is used, and the app tells you which language or accent it will use. The spoken text matches that voice’s language. If no supported offline voice is available, the app lets you know and keeps your voice preference. If voice was the only effective alert channel you chose, the app blocks starting or pauses the session until you enable another channel or explicitly turn off voice alerts. It does not download voices or use voices that require a network connection. Tabahiito does not send text or audio to any service operated by the developer.

## How to delete your data
**Settings > Reset data** deletes workouts, history, settings and the error log. They are also deleted when you uninstall the app. The temporary copy created when sharing the log is replaced the next time you export it and deleted the next time the app starts.

## Minors
Tabahiito is not intended for anyone under 18.
